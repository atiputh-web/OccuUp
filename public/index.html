How it works

From guest list to direct booking.

Four steps. Nothing is sent until the list is clean and you have signed.

01 · Upload

Export only what a campaign needs.

Sign up with your company email and a one-time code. Before any upload, you accept a data processing agreement. Columns we don't ask for are dropped automatically.

We ask for

  • First name
  • Email
  • Booking date
  • Check-in and check-out
  • Room type and rate
  • Country
  • Booking channel
  • Number of stays
  • Unsubscribe status

We never accept

  • Full name
  • Phone number
  • Date of birth
  • ID or passport number

02 · Free check

Nine rules clean the list before anyone pays.

Our own system runs these at no cost. You see totals on a dashboard. No guest names are shown or exported until you buy.

  1. 1Merge duplicates and tag returning guests. They are never removed.
  2. 2Remove malformed addresses.
  3. 3Fix obvious domain typos, such as gmial.com.
  4. 4Remove OTA relay addresses.
  5. 5Separate travel agent and wholesaler addresses.
  6. 6Remove fake, shared and disposable addresses.
  7. 7Remove guests who unsubscribed.
  8. 8Remove stays older than 8 years.
  9. 9Split recent stays (0–5 years) from older ones (5–8 years), which are sample-tested first.

If you don't buy, the original file is deleted within 30 days and you receive proof of deletion.

03 · Verify

An independent check of every address.

After you sign, addresses go to a verification service that has a data processing agreement with us. Only the email column is sent. It costs ฿0.25 per address checked, and the verified count sets your price tier.

04 · Win back

Sent from your hotel's own domain.

Low-season and long-stay offers go out under your name. Bookings land in your own booking engine, not an OTA.

Sending standards

Built to stay out of the spam folder.

6 DNS records to authenticate your domain with DKIM, SPF and DMARC. Your existing SPF record is left untouched.
1 click to unsubscribe from any email, as Gmail and Yahoo require of bulk senders.
0.05% complaint rate at which sending pauses automatically. Gmail's limit is 0.3%.
3% bounce rate at which sending pauses automatically for your hotel.

Measurement

We count only the bookings our emails added.

Some guests would come back anyway. So each campaign holds back a small random group that gets no email. The difference between the two groups is what OccuUp added.

Questions

What counts as a verified email?

An address the verification service marks valid (not invalid, unknown or catch-all), that isn't an OTA relay, shared or disposable address, has a PDPA basis for contact, isn't unsubscribed and isn't a duplicate. OTA guests count only if they gave consent to your hotel directly.

Why a fixed fee instead of commission?

Because the bookings are yours. A fixed fee means your cost doesn't rise when a campaign works. We report results against a holdout group of guests who received no email, so you see only the bookings our emails added.

What if my list is too small?

The free check shows the totals before you pay anything. If the list is too small to cover the fee, we'll tell you, and suggest starting with check-in email capture instead.

Do email-only offers break my OTA rate parity?

They can. Some OTA contracts count added extras, such as free breakfast, as a lower price. We check each offer against the terms of your own contracts before anything is sent.
